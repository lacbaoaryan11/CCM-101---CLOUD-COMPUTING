# Mission 3 Reflection

Among AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, the cloud platform that impressed me the most is Amazon Web Services (AWS). I was impressed by the wide range of services it offers for computing, storage, networking, databases, and other cloud needs. Learning about AWS helped me understand how cloud platforms can support different types of applications and organizations.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide cloud computing services that allow users and organizations to use resources through the internet. All three platforms offer services for virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and Kubernetes. They also provide scalable infrastructure, allowing organizations to increase or decrease their resources depending on their needs.

Business requirements have an important role in selecting a cloud platform. Organizations need to consider their budget, existing technology, application requirements, scalability, security, and other business needs. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft technologies may benefit more from Azure, while an organization focused on artificial intelligence may prefer a platform with strong AI and machine learning services.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that I need to understand the client's actual problems and requirements before recommending a solution. Comparing the services of AWS, Azure, and GCP also helped me develop my decision-making and cloud computing knowledge.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved because I added organized documentation, comparison tables, client recommendations, and Linux investigation results. The mission also helped me practice using Markdown and maintaining a meaningful project history through GitHub. Overall, this activity gave me a better understanding of how cloud technologies can be evaluated and matched to real-world business needs.
